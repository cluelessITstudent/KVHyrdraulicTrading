ABOUT US

COMPANY PROFILE

Hydraulic, electrical, and mechanical service experience built from decades of hands-on work.

Who Are We?

Kingvish Hydraulic Trading has more than 23 years of experience in hydraulic works, providing professional repair, maintenance, troubleshooting, and technical support for hydraulic systems and heavy equipment.

Kingvish Hydraulic Trading was established on February 25, 2015 as a single proprietorship. We specialize in providing services for repair and maintenance of hydraulic systems.

We also provide services for automobile troubleshooting, starter repair, alternator repair, and other electrical or mechanical problems.

History

Kingvish Hydraulic Trading traces its roots to DIOME Auto Electrical, established in 1995 as a specialist in automotive electrical services for light and heavy vehicles, including starter and alternator repair, rewiring, and electrical troubleshooting.

Through years of technical expertise and continuous development, the company expanded its capabilities into hydraulic system repair and maintenance.

Following successful service delivery and growing customer demand, hydraulic solutions became a core part of the business, leading to the establishment of Kingvish Hydraulic Trading — a trusted provider of both hydraulic and automotive electrical services.
